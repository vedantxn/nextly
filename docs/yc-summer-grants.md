YC Summer Grants 2026
We want technical college students to spend the summer of 2026 building interesting technical projects full-time in SF, so we’re giving them grants to do it. We hope this will encourage the smartest students to indulge their intellectual curiosity instead of doing intern grunt work. Now is the best time ever to do this — AI models keep pushing the limits of what's possible to build, and AI tools are rapidly making programmers more productive. The returns to spending a summer building things at the edge of your programming ability have never been higher.

The YC summer grants be $20,000 in cash and $90,000 in compute credits from Amazon AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google GCP. You will also be invited to attend YC Startup School in June and we’ll hold events throughout the summer for you to meet and build community with the other grant recipients. You will need to be in San Francisco for the summer working exclusively on your project to receive the grant.

We’re most interested in funding technical projects using AI in new ways but we’re happy to fund anything that smart engineers find interesting. We’re not expecting the projects to be commercial or make money, though it’d be fine if they did.

The grants are a good way to explore if your project ideas might be worth pursuing as a startup after the summer. If your project is already a startup idea, and you are ready to work on it, you should apply to YC instead.

If this sounds interesting, you can apply by submitting your application below. We are doing rolling applications.

FAQ

Are these grants an alternative to applying to YC?

No. If you are ready to work on a startup now, you should apply to YC with your idea. These grants are for students who aren’t ready to do a startup yet and want the freedom to work on an interesting technical project this summer. We expect some of these projects will turn into startups but it’s totally fine with us if they don’t.

Who is eligible to apply for the grants?

Any undergraduate computer science or engineering college student, including freshmen and students outside the US. We’ll consider undergraduate students with different majors if they can show us they’re extremely technical e.g. impressive open source contributions. You have to be committed to working on your project exclusively throughout the summer.

Can I apply with a team?

We’re giving the grants to individuals. If you plan on working with someone else, you should each apply individually and let us know in the application that you’ll be working together.

What does a successful project look like?

We’ll consider anything that smart programmers would be impressed by as a successful project. This might be a developer tool you built for yourself or contributions to open source. Building something with users would be cool but we’re not expecting that.

Will there be classes and programs to attend?

No. Our goal is to let you work on your own projects independently and meet some other smart students. We’ll host events throughout the summer for you to build community with the other grant recipients and meet YC partners and alumni who can give advice on your projects.

Will you provide working space?

No, you should work out of where you are living.

Do I have to move to the Bay Area?

Yes, you’ll have to move since we’ll have events all summer at our offices in SF.

Can I still work an internship this summer?

No. The grant covers students to work full time on their project. We think the most ambitious students will find it more interesting to work on their own project though.

Do I need to set up a company to receive the grant?

No. The grants are given to you personally, you don’t need a company or business setup to receive them.

If you have any question about the Summer Fellows Grants, email fellows@ycombinator.com.
